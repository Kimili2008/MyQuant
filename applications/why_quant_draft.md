# Motivation Drafts

## Why quantitative finance

I am interested in quantitative finance because it combines the parts of Engineering Science I enjoy most: modelling uncertain systems, testing assumptions against data, and translating an abstract idea into a working implementation. My interest began through following US equities, but that experience also showed me how easy it is to confuse a persuasive story with robust evidence. I want to understand the statistical and market-structure reasons behind returns, risk, and model failure. This month I am building a momentum research framework and an option-pricing and hedging lab so that I can discuss both the mathematics and the practical limitations of financial models.

## Why Oxford Alpha Fund Quant

OAF Quant appeals to me because its public research process connects mathematical reasoning, data work, and an investment hypothesis. I would like to contribute as someone who brings engineering discipline to data validation and numerical implementation while continuing to develop financial intuition. I am especially interested in understanding how a simple signal survives realistic costs, changing regimes, and out-of-sample testing.

## Why CapitOx Finance Academy

CapitOx is attractive because the Academy gives students a structured way to learn finance through assignments, mentoring, and a pitch. I would bring a willingness to learn quickly and an engineering habit of breaking open-ended questions into assumptions, tests, and a clear conclusion. I am particularly interested in learning how analytical work becomes a decision that another person can challenge.
