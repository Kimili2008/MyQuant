# Interview Bank

Use this as a timed bank, not as a script. Exact past society questions are not public. The categories below follow the publicly disclosed OAF Quant process and the published Oxford Finance Society interview guide.

## Quant maths

1. What is the expected number of coin tosses before the first head?
2. Two dice are rolled. What is the probability that the second roll exceeds the first?
3. How would you calculate the expected maximum of two independent uniform random variables?
4. Explain the difference between independence and zero correlation.
5. If a signal has a 55% chance of being correct, when does repeated use become profitable after costs?
6. Derive the variance of a weighted sum of two random variables.
7. Explain Bayes' theorem in a medical-testing example.
8. Estimate the number of trading days in ten years.
9. Why does the sample mean become approximately Normal?
10. What changes when observations are autocorrelated?

## Programming and data

1. Compute daily and log returns from a price series.
2. Implement a rolling mean without using a finance library.
3. Calculate maximum drawdown and identify its start and end dates.
4. Explain why using today's close to trade at today's close can create look-ahead bias.
5. Handle missing prices without silently changing the portfolio universe.
6. Calculate portfolio volatility from a covariance matrix.
7. Explain the time and memory complexity of your implementation.
8. Write a function that validates that portfolio weights sum to one.
9. Explain vectorisation in pandas and when a loop is clearer.
10. Design tests for a backtest that must not use future data.

## Financial analysis

1. Walk through the three financial statements.
2. How are the statements connected?
3. What is Enterprise Value and how does it differ from Equity Value?
4. What is EBITDA and what can make it misleading?
5. Walk through a DCF.
6. When would multiples be preferable to a DCF?
7. What is WACC?
8. What is beta and how can it be estimated?
9. What is the difference between a future and a forward?
10. Explain call and put payoffs.
11. Explain put-call parity.
12. How does volatility affect an option price?
13. What happens to a bond price when its required yield rises?
14. Why might actual cash flow be below forecast?
15. What makes a backtest economically implausible?

## Motivation and behaviour

1. Tell me about yourself.
2. Why finance?
3. Why quantitative finance?
4. Why Oxford Alpha Fund Quant?
5. Why CapitOx Finance Academy?
6. What did your Engineering Science background teach you that helps in finance?
7. Tell me about an analytical project that failed.
8. Tell me about a time you changed your mind after seeing evidence.
9. Tell me about a difficult team situation.
10. What is one market event you have followed recently?
11. Give an investment view and the evidence that would disprove it.
12. What would you do if your model produced a very attractive backtest?

## Mock format

### OAF Quant style 60 minutes

- 25 minutes mathematical problem solving
- 20 minutes Python/data reasoning
- 15 minutes financial analysis

Score each answer on correctness, clarity, and time. A correct answer with no explanation is not a finished answer.

### Society / Academy interview 25 minutes

- 3 minutes introduction
- 5 minutes motivation
- 8 minutes market or company discussion
- 6 minutes technical problem
- 3 minutes questions for the interviewer
