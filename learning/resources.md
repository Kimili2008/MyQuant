## Optional later resources

- [Jane Street education](https://www.janestreet.com/what-we-do/education/) for probability games and market-making intuition.
- Xinfeng Zhao, *A Practical Guide to Quantitative Finance Interviews* after the first mock test.
- Shreve, *Stochastic Calculus for Finance II*, only after probability, linear algebra, and calculus are comfortable.
- C++ and systems programming after the first term if quant developer or high-frequency trading becomes the priority.

## Reading rule

For every chapter or lecture, write: `concept -> equation or algorithm -> 10-line implementation -> one limitation`. A resource is complete only when its output exists in this repository.
